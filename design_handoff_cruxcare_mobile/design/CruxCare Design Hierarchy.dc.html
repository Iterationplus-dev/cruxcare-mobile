RULES THIS STRUCTURE ENFORCES
One account, roles added
A therapist never registers twice. TA2 links the existing Crux ID; the client tabs stay available through TD8.
Money is disclosed before it moves
Fees, commission, lock periods, clearing times and resulting balances appear on the screen that takes the action, never after.
Destructive actions are reversible or named
AM2 states the exact date deletion becomes permanent; AM3 gives a one-tap undo. Log out warns about unsynced work.
Health data follows the booking
Only a therapist with a confirmed session sees the health profile. PR4 lists who holds access and can withdraw it.
Error states ship with their screen
CA3-e, CA4-e, TA3, TA3-r, PR6 and TD2 carry the failure, waiting and expiry cases inline rather than in a separate appendix.
Accessibility is not optional
Every text pair meets WCAG 2.1 AA, tap targets hold at 44px, and the three colour rules in the design system §11 govern any new screen.
