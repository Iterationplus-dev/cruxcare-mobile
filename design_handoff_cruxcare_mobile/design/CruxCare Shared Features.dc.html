Privacy Terms Cooperative rules
Version 2.1 · in force since 1 June 2026History
In short
Your health data is visible only to therapists you book and to nobody else, we never sell it, and you can export or delete everything from Personal information.

1 · What we collect

Account details you give us, your health profile answers, session and order records, payment records held by our provider, and device information needed to keep the account secure.

2 · Who can see your health profile

Only a therapist with a confirmed booking, for as long as that care relationship lasts. Access is logged, and you can withdraw it in Security and privacy.

3 · Money and the cooperative

Wallet, savings, commitment and ranking positions are recorded against your Crux ID. Your upline sees your name and tier, never your balances.
